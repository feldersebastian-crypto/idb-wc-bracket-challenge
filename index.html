Step 01 · The Rules

How It Works

Pick the top three teams in each of the 12 groups, fill out the knockout bracket from the Round of 32 through to the Final, name your Golden Boot winner, and call your shot on the Final score. Submit by kickoff on June 11, 2026. Highest cumulative score takes home the pot.

Step 01 · The Group Stage

Pick 1st, 2nd & 3rd in Each Group

Tap the 1, 2, or 3 button next to a team to mark it as the group winner, runner-up, or third-place finisher. Tap again to clear. On the next screen, you'll pick which 8 of your 12 third-place teams actually qualify for the Round of 32.

Step 02 · Third-Place Qualifiers

Pick the 8 Best 3rd-Place Finishers

In the 48-team format, the eight third-place teams with the best records across all 12 groups advance to the Round of 32. From the third-place teams you picked in each group, select the 8 that you think will advance. The remaining 4 will be eliminated.

You earn 1 point per correctly predicted 3rd-place qualifier. Each R32 matchup involving a "best 3rd" slot will automatically pull from the appropriate group based on FIFA's official bracket structure.
